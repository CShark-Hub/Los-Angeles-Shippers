# Code of conduct

Everyone who takes part in this project (in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and any other
space of the project) is expected to follow the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and considerate, welcome people of every background and level of
experience, give and take criticism about the work and not the person, and don't harass, insult or
demean anyone, publicly or privately. Don't publish anyone's private information.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, write to the maintainers privately: open a report on the
repository's **Security** tab and say that it is about conduct. Reports are read only by the
maintainers, handled in confidence, and answered. The maintainers decide on the consequences the
Covenant describes, from a private warning to a ban from the project.
